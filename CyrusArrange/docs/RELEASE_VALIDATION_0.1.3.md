## Practical limits

The tested workflow is select furniture, assign category, register compatible accessories, preview/generate, refine, save/reopen and reproduce from a seed. Support discovery is sampled; conservative upright accessory bounds can underfill tight spaces. Region exclusions control anchors; physical keep-clear areas require obstacle volumes. Furniture and accessory semantics/fronts remain artist-authored. A portable `.cyalib` contains geometry/metadata; appearance requires source scene data. Rendering evidence is Standard diffuse / Default Scanline, including a simplified transparent cover on the imported frame, not original PBR or arbitrary third-party renderers.

The two pre-entry Max startup failures remain preserved with unresolved cause; offline stack evidence is in [startup diagnostics](STARTUP_DIAGNOSTICS.md). Later complete exact-source campaigns pass, but neither that history nor these fixtures establishes zero defects on every model/machine. Relational dining place settings, leaning/physics and broad automatic aesthetic judgment remain explicitly deferred extensions. Performance was measured with artist sessions open and uncontrolled background activity; it is not viewport FPS or an idle-machine baseline.

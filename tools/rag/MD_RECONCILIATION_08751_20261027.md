# Knowledge Markdown reconciliation — 27 October 2026

## Scope and result

This task inventories **all 39 Markdown parents** in the bound corpus v43 and
reconciles the relevant maintained navigation, product and Library guidance with
the delivered **0.87.5.1 Max 2027** baseline. This is not semantic certification
of every historical research claim or all repository Markdown. The baseline is
identified from delivery evidence, not the moving development version.

Ten existing maintained Markdown files were updated, including nine enrolled
parents and the knowledge entrypoint. Main corrections:

- Native Scene preset / Portable preset / Settings only replace the old asset
  checkbox workflow in the maintained Library owner guide.
- Scene presets retain native setup state and collect declared assets; they
  require matching units and installed dependencies. Standard merge clears Undo
  history with the short notice explicitly selected by the user.
- Portable format 9 and Edit/storage 3 carry their supported authored state;
  static adapters do not become arbitrary scene serializers. Copy limitations,
  receiver compatibility and interaction stack limits remain explicit.
- Product status now points to the delivered 0.87.5.1 package, independently
  versioned Analyzer/content and earlier hosts' separate qualification.
- Curved-surface Paint availability is corrected against the 0.86.6 delivery.
  Direct surface painting is distinct from constrained recipe Paint transfer.
- Broken references to a nonexistent 0.87.4.4 delivery report now point to its
  actual plan/artist scope instead of presenting them as an exact test receipt.
- The versioned 0.87.1 shared-store guide has a successor notice; its original
  storage details and historical qualification are retained.

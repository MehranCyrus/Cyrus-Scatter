# Recording useful lessons

Use this when a completed investigation, correction, review or explicit decision
produces knowledge that is likely to prevent repeated work. It is a record format,
not a new database, an automatic ingestion process or authorization to publish.

## Choose the appropriate record

- Put an established behavior or workflow correction in its maintained document
  when documentation is part of the authorized work.
- Keep a dated reproduction and exact verification in the relevant task report.
- Put a proposed explanation, unresolved conflict or suggested rule in the handoff
  as a candidate finding. Do not describe it as an accepted decision.
- Change AGENTS.md or a skill only for a durable workflow requirement supported by
  the task or a demonstrated recurring failure. Avoid appending session history.

Avoid duplicate entries for the same lesson. Link existing evidence, record what
supersedes an earlier conclusion, and retain the earlier record's historical scope.
Do not copy conversations, secrets, private assets or large logs into a lesson.

## Minimum useful content

Scale the record to its significance; a short paragraph may be enough. Include:

1. **Claim or decision:** what was learned or explicitly decided, and why it matters.
2. **Trigger and scope:** component, version/build, relevant settings, ownership and
   conditions under which it applies. Include known exclusions.
3. **Evidence:** source path and exact range; relevant full-parent/build identity;
   reproduction, command and result, or a link to the retained receipt.
4. **Status and review:** distinguish a hypothesis, reproduced observation,
   agent-reviewed proposal, and explicit human decision. Record who actually
   reviewed what and when; do not invent reviewer identities or approval.
5. **Limits and invalidation:** what was not tested and what change would require
   revisiting the claim. Identify conflicts or superseded records where relevant.

An observation can be useful without human approval when accurately labeled.
An agent's successful test does not make a product decision human-approved.
Conflicting or later evidence can reduce confidence or invalidate a prior claim.

## Keep recording separate from retrieval publication

Editing an enrolled document changes its full-parent hash, even outside indexed
ranges. Mention that consequence in the handoff. A new lesson may also need explicit
enrollment and evaluation evidence before a future candidate can retrieve it.

Record the lesson within the current task's documentation scope. Candidate
preparation, label changes and activation follow their own existing authorization
and review requirements. Do not edit a live index, insert chat history, or mark a
candidate accepted merely to make new learning searchable immediately.

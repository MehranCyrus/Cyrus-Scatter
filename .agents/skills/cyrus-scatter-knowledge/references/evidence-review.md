# Evidence review and publication

Use this for corpus edits, conflicting sources, semantic qualification or a
knowledge publication. It is conditional detail, not routine startup reading.

Check explicit enrollment, full-parent identity, selected ranges, canonical and
duplicate provenance, evidence role, historical status and applicability. Fresh
bytes may contain obsolete claims. A source test is not a passing result. Read
originals and surrounding context before changing a consequential claim.

For required behavior route to the appropriate F01–F12 contract. Implementation
requires the current source/ownership path; execution requires exact command,
input and output identity; loaded acceptance requires the matched host witness.
Report direct-source fallback separately from retrieval success. Preserve whole
contract guarantees, exceptions, limits and falsifiers. A missing indexed result
does not prove nonexistence.

Preserve P/I/PG/N identifiers, independent products and qualification labels.
The execution ledger owns current roadmap status; historical proposals cannot
authorize future work. Standalone Physics research and public Scatter integration
have separate evidence. Never rank authority by recency or query rank alone.

Editing any part of an enrolled parent changes its identity. Check scope before
editing a concurrent generation; do not hide drift with hash repair. Candidate
preparation, range/status review, evaluations and consumer activation are separate
explicit stages. Preserve originals, frozen questions and historical receipts.
Freshness does not constitute human semantic acceptance or answer accuracy.

Use the actual advertised schema. For errors consult the existing adapter
contract only as needed: bound retries, retain successful results and distinguish
stale/incompatible/corrupt/denied/incomplete evidence. Do not broaden access,
resource limits, watchers or registrations to conceal failure.

Record verified reusable lessons in their maintained owner or dated report, with
cause, scope, evidence, uncertainty and invalidation conditions. Do not turn chat
history, secrets or private artist assets into training data or established facts.

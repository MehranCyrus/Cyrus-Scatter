## Budgets and evidence

Design enrollment remains at most three mesh sources, three regions/layers, 2,000 aggregate requested candidates, two successful applications and two captures, with 24 ordinary calls per scope and specific outcome/retry recovery exceptions. Native ten-population capacity is distinct from the 32-entry inspection guard. Container reads cap 32 references and 1,024 registered rows per set; presentation strings are bounded. Native source following caps the whole captured hierarchy at 1,024 nodes and ancestor traversal at 64.

Plan size is 32 KiB, context 64 KiB; capture long edge 1,536 pixels and base64 payload 2 MiB. Execution-record export is exactly 1,500,000 bytes. Journal retains 128 records. Publication paging has 2,048 attempts per enrollment, including failed/stale host attempts. Design site/assets retain explicit triangle/vertex and static-controller admission checks.

Do not carry limits into a new feature by assumption or evade them through repeated enrollment. Use [the 0.73 results](../Unified_System_0.73_2026-10-06/RESULTS.md) for exact source/binary campaigns. Offline tests, mocked lifecycle checks, API-driven Max fixtures, actual pointer behavior and presented-frame measurements are different evidence classes.

## Source assessment

- Snapshot geometry, pose, options and revisions are copied into immutable entries sorted by stable caller IDs. No mutable host world or engine BodyID is used as caller identity.
- Every admitted obstacle receives a native query. Independent primitive refinement covers solid containment and the full segment; incomplete/ambiguous/over-budget queries discard staged results and do not become clear.
- Capsule local +Y is explicitly aligned to the directed span. Pair translations subtract the copied obstacle center in double before float conversion. Qualified material u, outward normal, primitive-surface membership and once-applied clearance are separate from raw hits.
- Member and local declaration order release shapes before the final runtime lease. The standalone owner serializes global registration and supports multiple worlds. This does not establish DLL coexistence with the existing Max PhysicsLab runtime.
- Query and construction limits, numeric admission and cooperative cancellation are explicit. Already-set cancellation is tested; interruption during native work, mutex-wait latency and forced OOM are not qualified.

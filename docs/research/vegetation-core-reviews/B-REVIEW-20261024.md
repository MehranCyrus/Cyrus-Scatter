## Source assessment

- Every call starts from immutable rest. Rest identity, semantic ordering, array counts, numeric/frame/envelope admission and generation are checked before solving. There is no deformed warm-start history.
- Whole-span contacts are refreshed before response and after all length/proposal/frame changes. Local witness algebra and identity are checked; all admitted obstacle rows are sorted deterministically. B relies on the backend for primitive surface qualification because its interface does not expose obstacle geometry.
- PositionOnly and ClampFrame have distinct angular behavior. A genuine zero movable contact gradient returns a local conflict; tiny positive coefficients are scaled before division so they are not mistaken for exact zero through squaring underflow.
- The fitter installs a private pose only after a complete final audit. Interrupted or incomplete successor scans retain the previous fully audited diagnostic pose. Unknown penetration/residuals remain explicitly unknown and cannot certify clearance.
- Numerical proposal size, loops, query counts and controlled payloads are bounded. Frame transport preserves admitted incremental roll and rejects unsupported antipodal/full-turn charts. These choices do not imply a calibrated elastic rod.

## Exact-source facts that affect this adapter

### Capsule placement and coordinate frames

[CapsuleShape.h, lines 20–30 and 47–48](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Collision/Shape/CapsuleShape.h#L20-L48) places the cap centers at `(0, ±halfHeightOfCylinder, 0)`: its longitudinal axis is **local +Y**. For segment `a→b`, use cylinder half-height `|b-a|/2`, midpoint translation and a rotation mapping +Y to the directed span. The contract's **material +Z tangent** is a distinct frame convention; it must not be passed to the capsule unchanged. Direct capsule construction requires positive half-height and radius. The settings factory explicitly substitutes a sphere for zero half-height in [CapsuleShape.cpp, lines 54–68](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Collision/Shape/CapsuleShape.cpp#L54-L68); that does not authorize silently accepting a contract-degenerate segment.

[Shape.h, lines 209–210](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Collision/Shape/Shape.h#L209-L210) defines center-of-mass-relative shapes and a default zero COM. Spheres, boxes and capsules used here are centered primitives without a nonzero COM override. [TransformedShape.h, lines 92–118](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Collision/TransformedShape.h#L92-L118) separately exposes COM and creation-space transforms; these must not be conflated when the profile eventually expands.

[TransformedShape.cpp, lines 65–93](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Collision/TransformedShape.cpp#L65-L93) verifies the actual public path: point queries transform into local coordinates before converting to float; shape queries subtract the supplied double base offset before `ToMat44`, then call `CollisionDispatch::sCollideShapeVsShape`. Contact positions returned by that path are relative to the chosen base offset. Choosing a nearby base offset preserves local float precision; double world positions do not turn local shapes or contact calculations into double precision. [Architecture.md, lines 750–762](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Docs/Architecture.md#L750-L762) explicitly describes this mixed-precision design.

### Normal sign, clearance and witness qualification

[CollideShape.h, lines 59–67](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Collision/CollideShape.h#L59-L67) defines the penetration axis as the direction that moves **shape 2** out. With the query capsule as shape 1 and obstacle as shape 2, the query response normal is `-mPenetrationAxis.Normalized()`. Raw contact points are surface points; the structure supplies no material-span `u`. Projecting or labeling an arbitrary raw point as `u` is not sufficient qualification.

[CollideShape.h, lines 82–100](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Collision/CollideShape.h#L82-L100) distinguishes GJK collision tolerance, relative penetration tolerance and maximum separation distance. Nearby reported contacts can have negative penetration depth. Those settings are not interchangeable with geometric radius, artist clearance or an exact global signed-distance oracle.

[BoxShape.h, lines 21–29 and 42–44](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Collision/Shape/BoxShape.h#L21-L44) states that convex radius is subtracted internally before inflation, keeping the declared extent. It rounds the shape, rather than providing outward artistic clearance. This adapter requires explicit convex radius **zero** to match the contract's sharp OBB. Capsule radius is geometric radius plus clearance once; primitive signed-distance refinement itself subtracts neither.

### Solid point tests and complete enumeration

[SphereShape.cpp, lines 269–276](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Collision/Shape/SphereShape.cpp#L269-L276) tests local squared distance against squared radius. [BoxShape.cpp, lines 224–231](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Collision/Shape/BoxShape.cpp#L224-L231) tests every absolute local coordinate against its half-extent. Both include their boundary. These are volume tests for admitted primitives, not surface-hit tests.

The public query documentation warns that mesh point classification requires a closed manifold in [NarrowPhaseQuery.h, lines 37–40](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Collision/NarrowPhaseQuery.h#L37-L40). The fallback in [Shape.cpp, lines 288–320](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Collision/Shape/Shape.cpp#L288-L320) counts raw triangle ray hits. The historical shared-diagonal degeneracy must not be generalized into a defect in the primitive tests, nor used to admit meshes here. No surface hit is never a general outside certificate.

The module must examine every admitted obstacle, retain stable caller IDs independently of any engine IDs, and fail closed when cancellation, capacity or work limits prevent complete enumeration. A nearest-hit collector across the entire scene cannot meet that contract.

### Immutability and standalone process lifetime

[Architecture.md, lines 853–867](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Docs/Architecture.md#L853-L867) permits shapes to be shared across physics systems but scopes bodies/IDs/constraints to a system; factory, material, dispatch, allocation and debug hooks are shared. [RegisterTypes.cpp, lines 96–107 and 186–201](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/RegisterTypes.cpp#L96-L201) requires allocation/factory initialization, installs dispatch/types and clears factory/default material during unregistration. [Memory.cpp, lines 68–80](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Core/Memory.cpp#L68-L80) shows global allocation function pointers.

Consequently a world must not independently replace the global factory or unregister while another world, query or shape is alive. Use one standalone shared lifetime owner and destroy shapes before releasing its final ownership. This requirement does **not** establish safe coexistence with another independently linked Jolt copy, a foreign process owner or Max cross-DLL ABI compatibility.

Refcounting shape lifetime does not freeze poses. Copy both geometry and transforms for the world generation. Also note that [TransformedShape.cpp, lines 70–71 and 87–88](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Collision/TransformedShape.cpp#L65-L93) updates collector context and `ShapeFilter::mBodyID2`; [ShapeFilter.h, lines 44–46](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Collision/ShapeFilter.h#L44-L46) makes that field mutable. Query-local collectors and filters avoid shared state mutation even though obstacle shapes and poses are immutable.

**Allocation failure containment has a specific limit.** The module's `src/jolt_runtime.cpp` wraps the default allocators, throwing `std::bad_alloc` for an ordinary null allocation; a failed nonzero reallocation preserves the old block, and zero-size reallocation explicitly frees it. This does not make every engine container strongly exception-safe. In pinned [HashTable.h, lines 182–220](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Core/HashTable.h#L182-L220), `GrowTable` moves old allocation pointers to local variables and clears the table members before allocating the replacement. A throwing replacement allocation can therefore leave old storage unreachable. After runtime-initialization failure, the module releases reachable factory maps, default material and factory storage, restores its initially null allocator hooks, and poisons the owner. An ordinary allocation failure with completed reachable cleanup returns `AllocationFailure`; subsequent construction returns `InternalError` and requires process restart, preventing repeated uncertain leak accumulation. Failed cleanup itself returns `InternalError`. Full reclamation of unreachable engine storage, catastrophic-OOM recovery and throwing behavior through all engine `noexcept` paths are not promised. Registry storage, allocator bookkeeping and other Jolt internals remain outside exact per-world allocation instrumentation.

## Analytic refinement: scope, cost and numerical limits

`src/primitive_geometry.*` is separate application mathematics supplementing actual public-Jolt calls. A sphere uses its closest segment point. An OBB is transformed rigidly to a local AABB: outside squared distance is piecewise quadratic across six slab planes; inside signed distance is the upper envelope of six affine face distances. Evaluating the finite candidate set finds the minimum point signed distance along the entire segment. It uses fixed arrays, no heap allocations, and at most 32 candidate evaluations per box query.

The result supplies an actual axis parameter, axis point, outward unit normal and obstacle surface witness. Qualification checks reconstruction, surface membership, outward normal/cone membership and signed distance. The adapter then subtracts geometric radius and clearance exactly once. For point exclusion, `SDF(point)-exclusion_radius` is Euclidean expansion; expanding every OBB half-extent by the radius would instead introduce a conservative L-infinity envelope.

One deepest witness per obstacle is a deliberate reduction. It certifies the admitted primitive overlap/clearance question; it is not a full contact manifold, an exact rigid-capsule minimum translation vector, or proof that a fitted ribbon can move there. At a sphere center or box medial axis the outward direction is nonunique. Ties are deterministic, but normals can change discontinuously. A fitter must refresh contacts after finite proposals and still obtain the independent visible-surface validation required by the contract.

Numerical review found no additional helper change needed for a bounded admission profile. The implementation's `numerical_bound_m` is `1024*DBL_EPSILON` times a coordinate/extent/span scale: an explicit engineering allowance, **not a formal interval enclosure or a measured Jolt error**. Large absolute coordinates can lose small offsets during subtraction/reconstruction even in double; arbitrary huge inputs and features below meaningful precision must be rejected by admission. Require this allowance to fit inside the accepted query tolerance. The local quadratic calculations also rely on bounded span/coordinate magnitudes to avoid overflow. Near-unit quaternions are normalized only within the helper's declared `1e-8` norm admission; this does not accept shear or general affine scale. A locally consistent witness does not by itself prove the helper's minimizer is correct, so independent analytic controls/property checks remain necessary.

## Why no rod or compute-hair claim follows

The CPU-rod path and its particle contact response are not used. For example, [SoftBodySharedSettings.cpp, line 475](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/SoftBody/SoftBodySharedSettings.cpp#L475) derives rod inverse mass from the minimum endpoint inverse mass; that is relevant to the historical root-orientation trap, not an anchoring implementation in this adapter. The separate [Hair.h, lines 21–42](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/1b2e77e3c6c09709e5edc79dc208459b8ff32051/Jolt/Physics/Hair/Hair.h#L21-L42) describes a system still in development and a compute-system initialization path. Its documented missing shape support and CPU/GPU storage are not capabilities of the public rigid-query adapter. No result here claims a plant binding, solver, renderer or host implementation.

## Suggested sample selection

Use multiple geometry families and a realistic range of source complexity. Include at least one intentionally unsuitable source so failure reporting is evaluated. Do not infer a support percentage from a conveniently chosen small sample; record the selection method and intended population.

“Experiment-ready” means the inputs are sufficiently explicit for a proposed test. It does not establish a shipped feature or renderer qualification.

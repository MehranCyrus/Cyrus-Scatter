## Verification limits

Live vendor pages can change. Keep access dates and exact installed versions when executing a recipe. The checked/report-backed distinction above prevents this register from implying a fresh independent audit of every citation in all supplied originals.

The source manifest hashes the user-supplied documents, not remote webpages or vendor example binaries. HDA hashes printed in R09/R10 are the author's reported evidence; the corresponding downloaded-asset manifest was not supplied.

R11 differs: its source-preparation evidence ZIP is embedded in the supplied HTML. We preserved it, checked its printed hash and CRC, inspected its stored results and independently recounted the public mesh topology. [The review record](R11-evidence-review.json) distinguishes that work from the report author's 17 checks. The bundle's scripts were not executed here.

For R12, the [source review](R12-source-review.json) records eleven local/public file comparisons at one exact Jolt commit, including hashes and newline-normalized equality. P42 is an additional locally inspected path, not part of that eleven-file public comparison. These checks establish source behavior only. They do not identify a built/loaded engine or complete any proposed native/Max experiment.

R13's [evidence review](R13-evidence-review.json) records all 48 cited full-file hashes matching the local dependency after line-ending normalization, with 82 valid ranges. Its original ZIP retains the larger source index/excerpts and reported build/results. We inspected those data without running the harness; the supplied Linux diagnostics, our source checks and future Cyrus acceptance are separate evidence categories.

R14's [review](R14-evidence-review.json) records 49 local full-file matches, 86 valid ranges and a 94-entry report register. Its native/analytic evidence uses different labels and build scope from R13. The exact ZIP retains its build logs, supplied API/tag metadata, source excerpts and license. Those data were inspected without executing the bundled code. P48 also supports the shared-runtime service distinction in its multiple-physics-systems section; actual Cyrus module/build ownership remains a later audit.

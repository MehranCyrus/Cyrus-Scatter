## Repeatable engineering loop

1. Pin source, loaded modules, scene and expected behavior.
2. Reproduce both the real change and the unchanged idle state in a disposable profile.
3. Make one minimal dependency/lifecycle correction.
4. Check actual rows/epochs, errors, pending state, callback/timer work and uploads; include failed successors.
5. Rerun affected regression cases on a fresh frozen pair. Keep failed receipts.
6. Update the capability/help and evidence boundary, then stop expanding that slice once its acceptance is met.

Do not compensate for a failing idle invariant by disabling Live, swallowing errors, forcing evaluation from an inspector or continually polling the entire scene. Do not report all 241 controls as individually tested merely because their inventory and six editor topics are present.

# P0 numerical and cost gates

These admission values are recorded before the prototype cost sweep. They are provisional development limits, not artist-facing promises. P0 remains incomplete until accuracy, work and resource accounting pass together.

- Aggregate per field: at most 32 Paths, 64 Bands, 65,536 source memberships, 8,192 input vertices, 32,768 prepared offset levels and 2,000,000 retained contour vertices.
- Pure query: at most 262,144 segment visits. No Clipper calls or preparation writes during a query. A rejected query fails the successor; it must not publish a partial population.
- Retained-data ceiling: 128 MiB per prepared field. Construction has a 250-million-work-unit limit and a conservative 256 MiB scratch allowance; aggregate field reservations are capped at 512 MiB, including admitted old/staged fields. Reservations cover declared numeric containers rather than proving a hard maximum on Clipper allocator or process overhead. Display/GPU/fallback resources still need their own P4 accounting and measurements.
- The caller supplies physical quantum in system units. The numerical experiment uses 0.00001 world units, a four-quantum contour uncertainty zone and a minimum width such that eight quanta do not exceed 1/256 of the width. Host integration must convert a declared physical tolerance; it may not silently use this experiment's unit value in every scene.
- Accuracy remains normalized progress error <=1/256 and aggregate absolute contribution error <=.005 away from declared support uncertainty. Ambiguous intermediate levels widen the bracket; they are not an exemption. Complex curves may fail admission rather than silently lose precision.
- Baseline timing sweep: one cold run and 20 warm runs, median and nearest-rank p95, at 1k/10k/100k candidates and 1/8/32 Rounded flat Bands. Preserve the exact old library/header identity. A native timer/noise floor must be measured before the 10% regression threshold is used.
- Prototype interactive budgets, measured separately: cached field inspection at 100k candidates / eight simple Bands <=500 ms warm median and <=750 ms p95; preparation of that field <=500 ms warm median. At 32 simple Bands, cached inspection <=2 s median and <=3 s p95. These are CPU development gates on the current machine, not promises of total Max Update latency or presented FPS.
- P4 Point/Mesh reservations include immutable CPU snapshots, staging, owner data, palette values, SDK system copies, GPU payloads and simultaneous generations. Limits remain Point/radius 64 MiB per owner / 128 MiB process, Mesh 512 MiB per owner / 1 GiB process, at most 20 caches and 1,024 nonempty groups. Shader/upload failure and reservation-release witnesses pass in the owned 2027 fault build. Driver, Max conversion and allocator overhead remain outside these payload counters; process/VRAM and final overlay measurements are still required. A passing CPU query gate cannot conceal extraction, upload or fallback cost.

Use analytic rectangles and a separately queried finer-grid Clipper reference for concave shapes, then acute corners, holes, reversed winding, collapse, narrow/steep fields and aggregate overlap. The independent reference must not use the prepared level table. Full low/zero contribution, saturated mixtures, intentional Empty lottery outcomes and stable reorder behavior are required separately from geometry.

# Cyrus Scatter 0.87.4.2 — selected leaf preparation and editor navigation

**Status: scoped Max 2027 patch ready for artist testing.** This patch repairs the reported Neottopteris Nidus Bush05 workflow and the preparation viewer's pan/orbit relationship. Native Interactions advances to **10** for the matched script/native commands. Scene schema **62**, authored intent **1** and prepared-definition envelope **2** remain unchanged; independently versioned products remain separate.

## Cause and resulting behavior

The source has 125,845 vertices, 183,030 triangles and 4,756 complete connected pieces. At the screenshot's 50 mm Minimum stem and 1 mm Branch connection, the prior build found 155 guides, including 46 unresolved leaf guides. Isolate only hid geometry; Automatic cleared selected role overrides and still analyzed the whole source. The popup also discarded the failed analysis report. Its generic error called Piece 4097 a welded branching problem, although that piece is a roughly 7 mm bark detail.

The new **Select problem guides** control selects those 46 unresolved leaf pieces directly. **Prepare selected only** creates their guides and keeps all other pieces fixed. In isolation, the clearly labeled **Prepare isolated only** action uses the isolated set even with no active selection. Ordinary Automatic behavior on the full source remains a role reset. Sparse source-fingerprint-bound intent records the scope; no mesh is modified or remeshed, and all triangles/corner/material correspondences remain.

The first scope prototype exposed three shorter drooping leaves with reversed roots. The crown-voting stage now considers explicitly selected shorter leaves against the established longer-leaf reference endpoints. All 46 recorded roots then lay in the crown bounds, rather than at drooping tips. Unsupported one-guide errors now name the piece and current Minimum stem without diagnosing welded topology from inference failure alone.

Failed analysis retains its counts and inferred guides. Partial guide display respects isolation. Invalid selections/scopes are rejected without changing a complete draft; an invalid subsequent draft cannot replace the saved scene definition. Scope changes have one draft Undo step, and scene commit retains the existing atomic, host-thread Undo transaction.

The viewer keeps screen pan offsets separate from the framed orbit pivot, uses aspect-aware framing and cursor-centered zoom, and preserves world up. Visible Orbit/Pan modes support left dragging; right-drag and middle-button controls remain. Capture-loss handling clears navigation gestures, and ordinary hover no longer requests redraws. Navigation does not reprepare sources or upload unchanged geometry in the recorded test.

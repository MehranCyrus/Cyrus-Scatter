## What changed

Connectivity-derived tube sections and geodesic ribbon charts supplement ordered stem/blade templates. Preparation now handles the supplied triangulated grasses, fronds and separated plant pieces. Indexed branch searches replace exhaustive guide-pair searches. Ground inference distinguishes planted long structures from detached short foliage; admissible short pieces remain rigid attachments with their original faces. Unresolved structures retain their report and cannot be accepted.

Advanced exposes source vertices/triangles, connected pieces, guides, preview triangles, estimated preparation memory, charged work and branch attachment reach. Defaults are 2M vertices / 4M triangles and 8,192 guides; maxima are 8M / 16M and 32,768 guides. Other limits and independent proxy/output bounds are documented in the artist guide. Higher allowances remain bounded by memory and representation checks.

Visual capture provenance now excludes collider-only box/custom captures. Saved reviewed definitions have their own SHA-256 cache identity, so a prior inferred rig or another accepted definition cannot replace them. Preview density reuses reviewed inference. The float32 1% endpoint is admitted correctly. Rooted contact searches multiple directions for short curved guides and freshly validates each candidate. Existing fixed-root and full-visible-mesh checks remain in place.

React activation checks readiness before changing roles. Source captions identify saved guides or missing preparation. Primitive spheres/boxes are rejected by the tree trunk heuristic with shape guidance. Explicit unused collider-link cleanup keeps the scene meshes. Preparation failures and failed contact successors retain the preceding complete publication.

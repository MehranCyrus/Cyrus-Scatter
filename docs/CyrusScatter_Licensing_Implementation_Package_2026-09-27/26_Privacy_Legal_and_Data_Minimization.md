# 26 — Privacy, Legal, and Data Minimization

This is engineering guidance, not legal advice.

## Collect only what is needed

Possible licensing data:
- license/customer ID;
- email/account identity if commerce requires it;
- machine fingerprint/digest;
- activation timestamps;
- product/release version;
- IP/provider logs as generated by vendor;
- seat/lease activity.

Avoid collecting scene names, geometry, asset paths or project content for licensing.

## Disclose

Privacy notice should explain:
- licensing provider;
- commerce/MoR;
- device/activation data;
- purpose;
- retention;
- support diagnostics;
- international processing where relevant.

## Contracts

Before launch review:
- provider DPA/security terms;
- MoR seller terms;
- refund/chargeback responsibilities;
- code-signing certificate terms;
- EULA/license grant;
- maintenance/update terms;
- offline/floating limitations.

## Customer trust

Do not silently introduce analytics under the label of “licensing.” Keep optional product telemetry separate from required license operations.

# Stage 05 — Define the product

## Goal

Choose a primary user, a practical product promise and three lead workflows that current evidence can support.

## Why this matters

A focused promise tells engineering what to improve and tells you what to demonstrate. Trying to lead with every feature makes the first experience difficult to explain.

## What we currently know

**VERIFIED IN SOURCE:** Cyrus combines surface placement, rules, layers, Analyzer-derived borders/paths and persistent edit records. **PROPOSED positioning:** precise environment/site layout that survives revisions and handoff. This follows the [governing product strategy](../Product_Strategy_2026-09-29/03_Product_Positioning_and_Workflows.md).

The strongest candidates are controlled courtyard planting, roadside/boundary objects and composed landscapes with local edits. Production handoff is a valuable goal whose renderer/asset/license behavior still needs qualification. “Large scatter editing,” “general site planning,” and “works with every renderer” are too broad without defined inputs and tests.

## Boss / Product-owner decisions

Approve the initial audience and offered scope before public positioning or pricing. Your draft can use archviz/environment artists as the working hypothesis while tests continue.

## Step-by-step procedure

1. Describe a real job: “The architect changed the planter border; I must revise trees, preserve hero placements and deliver a correct image.”
2. Score the candidates below using completed tests: useful, understandable, reliable, repeatable, and feasible to support. Use Low/Medium/High with evidence; do not turn uncertain scores into market statistics.
3. Choose one workflow that can lead a short demonstration and two supporting workflows.
4. Write the promise using concrete actions and outputs.
5. Ask the owner to choose among prepared scope options, with the missing evidence visible.
6. Update Stage 08's scene briefs and the claims matrix to match.

## Completion criteria

Your draft names a specific user/task, three workflows and explicit limits. Owner approval is recorded before public use. Missing runtime evidence is assigned, not hidden in aspirational wording.

## Do not do yet

Do not promise the plugin is indispensable for every artist, or fund a full UI rewrite, node graph, marketplace or AI feature without an observed workflow need.

## Optional / later

Extend into studios, animation, USD and enterprise deployment when repeated pilot demand and engineering evidence justify it.

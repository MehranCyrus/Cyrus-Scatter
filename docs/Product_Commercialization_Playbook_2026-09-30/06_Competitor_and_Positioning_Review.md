# Stage 06 — Competitor and positioning review

## Goal

Understand where Cyrus fits by comparing complete artist tasks, with verified facts and unknowns visible.

## Why this matters

Competitors already have broad scattering features. A useful position must be based on the task an artist finishes and the effort to maintain the result.

## What we currently know

The following **verified competitor facts** come from official pages opened on 2026-09-30. They establish vendor-documented capabilities, not an independent performance comparison.

| Product | Verified competitor fact | Cyrus verified capability | Unknown | Potential differentiator |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chaos Scatter | Official guidance documents slope/altitude, editing, clustering/painting and edge trimming | Source has masks, layers, boundary falloff, Analyzer data and edit records | Matched task time, revision behavior and exact tested versions | Combined site-analysis and controlled layout workflow, if pilots value it |

Cyrus's PFlow code does not establish a tyFlow integration. An overlapping feature is not evidence that a competitor cannot complete the same task. No provider or competing product was purchased/installed for this review.

## Step-by-step procedure

1. Select W1 courtyard revision, W2 boundary rows and W3 handoff from Stage 05.
2. Define common geometry, source asset complexity, units, count/clearance, acceptable appearance, edit task and renderer.
3. Record setup, revision, troubleshooting and render preparation separately.
4. Let a competent user of each tool complete the task; document help and assumptions.
5. Check that both outputs satisfy the same constraints. “Cannot reproduce with our knowledge” is not “competitor cannot do it.”
6. Record the result and choose a statement supported by it, such as an observed setup/revision benefit in the named configuration.
7. If tools/operators are unavailable, finish with a documented research plan and provisional positioning.

No licensing implementation or commercial release is claimed.

## Direction

Keep the native authority, signed permissions, scene preservation, generated UI, minimal data collection and separation from placement/viewport work. Use a test backend, then our service; the earlier Cryptlex/Keygen selection sequence is retired. The [document audit](../licensing/DOCUMENT_AUDIT.md) explains each correction.

Start with one complete authoring feature set and qualify individual/assigned studio seats before adding floating concurrency. Purchased quantities and discounts belong to the server catalogue and entitlement ledger. New same-product workflows reuse rights while their actual native mutation entry points receive coverage tests.

## First implementation work

Follow L0 and L1 in the roadmap: prove the native operation boundary, then build an isolated policy/signature harness. Trace changed script parameters, direct calls, evaluation, editing, baking and rendering. A process name or a caller-supplied render flag does not establish authority.

The owned service, native candidate, floating tests, commerce and qualified release follow those results. Existing Scatter/Analyzer builds and viewport gains do not establish licensing correctness.

## Business and continuity decisions

Use the single decision register for commercial model, devices, trial, offline duration, local/worker render rights, transfer, pricing and support. The former 30-day trial, one-device default and maintenance/offline examples are not approved terms.

Preserve saving, IDs and stored edits. Define faithful evaluation, local rendering, worker rights and expiry behavior explicitly before enforcement. An eligible perpetual build, an active maintenance term and a still-valid proof of entitlement are different facts. Do not promise permanent disconnected use while enforcing undisclosed periodic renewal.

Do not market air-gap servers, floating concurrency or an enterprise SLA before their deployment, recovery and support paths pass qualification. Prices require actual operating/support costs and the seller's applicable arrangements; neither legal conclusions nor payment availability follow from a workstation timezone.
